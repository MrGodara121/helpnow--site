# 🚨 HelpNow - 24/7 Emergency Home Services

## 📋 About
HelpNow connects users with licensed emergency professionals instantly. 24/7 emergency service, free estimates, insurance claims assistance.

### Services Offered
- 🔧 Emergency Plumbing (Burst pipes, leaks, backups)
- 💧 Water Damage Restoration (Flood, extraction, drying)
- 🏠 Roofing Emergency (Storm damage, leaks, tarp)
- ⚡ Electrical Emergency (Power outage, sparks)
- ❄️ HVAC Emergency (No heat/AC, gas leak)
- 🔥 Fire Damage Restoration (Smoke cleanup)
- ⚖️ Insurance Claim Help (Public adjuster)
